03 Where the money goes

Three profit leaks I plug.

The call nobody picked up

A lead rings during a delivery, at lunch, after hours. It goes to voicemail and then to your competitor. A voice agent answers in three seconds, every time, and books the appointment straight into your calendar.

Leak — enquiries you already paid for

The copy-paste tax

Someone on your payroll moves the same numbers between a sheet, a bill book and WhatsApp — every single day. It is slow, it is expensive, and it is where the mistakes come from.

Follow-ups, posts, reports, reminders. They get done in a good week and skipped in a busy one, and nobody notices until a month has gone quiet. Two of my clients said exactly this, unprompted.

Leak — revenue lost to things silently not happening

06 How it works

Audit, build, and then I keep it alive.

Most agencies sell you the middle step and leave. The third one is the reason my clients’ systems are still running.

  1. Audit

    A free conversation where I find the bottleneck. I look at how the work actually moves through your business, not at what software you own. If automation is the wrong answer, I will say so.

  2. Build

    I sketch the system, you approve it, I build it into how your team already works. No rip-and-replace, no six-month rollout, no retraining everyone on a new tool.

  3. Keep it alive

    APIs change, prices change, your business changes. I monitor what I build and fix it before you notice. This is a monthly plan, and it is the difference between a system and a demo.
